About

I spent five years at Tata Consultancy Services working on both ends of enterprise technology: building AI/ML and robotics prototypes for retail clients, and modernizing core banking platforms for a US bank as a full-stack developer.

That gave me a close-up view of where digital transformation projects actually stall, and it's rarely the technology itself. It's almost always the people, process, and change management around it. That's the problem I'm now studying formally, and the one I want to work on next.

Based in Aachen, Germany. Open to working student, internship, and thesis roles across NRW and wider Germany/Europe.
